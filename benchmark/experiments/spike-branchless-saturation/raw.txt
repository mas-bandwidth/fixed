  fixClamp / fixMin / fixMax : 0 jcc (clang and gcc) -- cmov.

                               rewrite; gcc 13.3 does not.

Latency improves on clang in-range (~1.3x) but regresses at 50% saturation (0.76x); gcc
shows no latency win at all (the rewrite is not branchless under gcc). Throughput REGRESSES
on both compilers (0.60-0.81x) because the branchless form executes the double->int64
conversion unconditionally every call, where the branchy form early-exits on a
perfectly-predicted in-range branch.
